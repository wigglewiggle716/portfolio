About

Who I am and how I think about marketing

I approach marketing strategically from start to finish. I begin by analysing the brand and defining its position in the market, then I build the Positioning plan and define the exact Target Audience. This determines the shape of the Content Strategy that fits the audience, connecting it to the Marketing Funnel and Customer Journey so I can understand how the customer moves from first discovering the brand to making a purchase.

I continuously analyse results and prepare a backup plan for problems that may appear during Strategy execution, with the flexibility to adjust any part of it. I also connect online and offline marketing, from managing social pages and campaigns to overseeing offline channels such as events, bazaars, signage and print collateral — keeping every brand touchpoint consistent wherever it appears.

I work across the full marketing mix — starting with brand analysis and positioning, moving through target audience and content strategy, all the way to funnel design, customer journey mapping, and continuous results analysis — always with a backup plan ready.
